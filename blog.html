CUSTOM SOCKS GUIDE

What to Include in a Custom Socks Quote Request

Clear project details help us recommend a suitable construction, estimate the cost, and prepare an accurate sample.

1. Tell us how the socks will be used

Sports, retail, corporate gifts and studio grip socks can call for different yarns, lengths and features. Share the target customer and preferred sock style.

2. Send the artwork and design preferences

Attach your logo, pattern or a reference image. Include colors, placement, sizes and any packaging ideas. A rough concept is enough to start the discussion.

3. Add quantity and timing

Tell us the estimated number of pairs, size breakdown, delivery destination and target date. Our standard starting MOQ is 500 pairs; final details depend on the design and materials.
