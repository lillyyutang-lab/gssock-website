Custom Sock Manufacturing in Zhuji, China

How GoodStartSocks helps brands, retailers and teams develop custom socks from a first idea through sampling, bulk production and packaging.

Factory-direct custom sock development

GoodStartSocks is an OEM and ODM custom sock manufacturer based in Zhuji, Zhejiang, China. Since 2010, our team has supported B2B sock programs for brands, retailers, teams and promotional projects.

We work from the project brief through sample development and bulk production. Customers can discuss sock type, yarns, sizing, logo placement, labels, packaging and delivery requirements with one team.

What we can help you plan

A clear brief helps turn an idea into a practical product specification. We can discuss everyday, sports, grip, compression, fashion and gift sock projects, along with custom colors, jacquard patterns, artwork, retail labels, boxes and gift-ready presentation.

For packaging inspiration, visit our custom sock project examples. For a guide to the key details needed before pricing, see what to include in a quote request.

Production information for new projects

  • Standard starting MOQ: 500 pairs
  • Sample preparation: about 3–7 days
  • Bulk production: about 30 days after sample approval
  • Capacity: approximately 50 knitting machines

Timing and final specifications depend on the construction, materials, artwork, size breakdown, packaging and order quantity. We recommend approving a sample before bulk production.

Start with your idea

Send your logo or reference image, intended sock type, estimated quantity, preferred materials, size range, packaging idea and target delivery date. Our team can use those details to discuss the next steps for your custom project.
