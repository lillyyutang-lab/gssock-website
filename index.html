Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ?

Our standard MOQ is 500 pairs per style and color. Final requirements can vary by construction, yarn and customization.

Can G&S manufacture socks under my brand?

Yes. We support private label production including custom logos, labels, hangtags and packaging.

What customization options are available?

Material, yarn blend, size, length, color, pattern, cushioning, logo method and packaging can be developed around your project.

Can I send an existing sock for development?

Yes. A physical reference sample or specification sheet can be used to evaluate construction and develop a production proposal.
